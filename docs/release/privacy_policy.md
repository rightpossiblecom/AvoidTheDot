# Privacy Policy — AvoidTheDot

Last updated: May 19, 2026

AvoidTheDot ("we", "us", or "our") provides a mobile game and related marketing website. This policy describes how information is handled when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location for sale or analytics.

The App stores gameplay statistics and preferences only on your device using local storage (for example, your best survival time and display settings). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it yourself outside the App (for example, by capturing a screenshot).

If you contact us by email, we receive your message and email address only so we can respond. We do not use that information for marketing lists.

## How we use information

On-device stats are used to show your personal bests inside the App. Settings such as theme choice are used to make the App look the way you expect.

Support emails are used only to help you with the App.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not run in-App analytics or advertising SDKs in this product. We may share information if required by law or to protect our rights.

## Children

The App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described here. If you believe we have received information from a child in error, contact us and we will delete it where appropriate.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
